# Case A, dark matter: answer key v2

Evidence cut-off 8 November 2026.

Why this case. Several rival programmes inside one domain, recent consensus documents, and overlap with the apparatus's own source (Branahl 2025), which discusses BSM physics and dark matter as candidates for stagnant status.

## Individuation

Programmes are individuated as dark-matter candidates inside the ΛCDM framework. Each shares the framework core (general relativity, a non-baryonic cold component with Ω_c h² ≈ 0.12) and has a hard core of its own: the nature of the constituent, its production mechanism and its positive heuristic for detection.

ΛCDM is the host framework and is not ranked. MOND and its relativistic extensions (AeST) are rivals to the framework, not to the candidates, and are not ranked either. They are expected on axis 5 only, as the main alternative to the shared core; their best current test, Gaia wide binaries, favours Newtonian gravity in the cleanest samples (Cookson et al. 2026), though the question stays open. A run that ranks ΛCDM or MOND among the candidates makes a level error, scored under fabrication.

Two boundary calls. Fuzzy dark matter is a sub-case of the axion/ALP programme and self-interacting dark matter a property any candidate can carry, so neither counts as a programme here, and a run that treats one as such is neither credited nor penalised. Branahl (v3, section 4.1) treats SUSY and non-SUSY models as supplements within one BSM protective belt, which argues against individuating by candidate. The key follows Duerr & Wolf (2023), who read ΛCDM as a framework model, and individuates by candidate because that is the level at which experiments and the consensus documents are organised.

## Programmes and expected verdicts

| # | Programme | Verdict | Principal pathology | Grounds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | QCD axion (haloscopes, helioscopes, LSW) | defensible | none | Motivated independently of dark matter (strong CP). Benchmark couplings (KSVZ, DFSZ) reached only by cavity haloscopes, in narrow windows between 4 and 37 μeV (CAPP 2024, QUAX 2024, ADMX 2025, HAYSTAC 2025); the dielectric, LSW and helioscope families are not yet at the QCD band (MADMAX 2025, ALPS II 2025, CAST 2017). Reservations: tiny mass coverage, one instrument family at benchmark, strong-CP motivation contested (Branahl v3, fn. 10). |
| 2 | Sub-GeV hidden-sector dark matter (dark-photon portal) | defensible | none (overfit is the risk to name) | Hard core: light mediator, thermal relic. Heuristic: thermal targets. Independent methods (Skipper-CCDs, xenon ionisation-only, fixed target, CMB). The 2025 results remove most elastic scalar, asymmetric and freeze-in targets (DAMIC-M, PandaX-4T, Cheek et al. 2025); resonant, Majorana and inelastic variants survive, with near-term tests (SuperCDMS from 2026, LDMX from 2027). The retreat towards those variants is the overfit risk. |
| 3 | Primordial black holes | inconclusive (evidence) | none (memory-burden sub-variant: untestability) | Asteroid-mass window, 10^17 to 10^22 g, still open and closing for wide mass functions (Gorton & Green 2024, Carr et al. 2026). No positive evidence. Memory-burden windows below the Hawking bound rest on an untested conjecture. |
| 4 | WIMP (thermal relic, incl. SUSY neutralino and higgsino) | fragile | ad-hocness | Decades of null results; LZ 4.2 t·yr then 417 live days, XENONnT 3.1 t·yr; the neutrino fog is reached. LHC: gluinos and squarks excluded to about 2.4 TeV, electroweakinos to about 1 TeV (ATLAS 2026). Heuristic shifted to higgsino and inelastic variants. The single non-blind 2.6σ LZ event of September 2026 is contested and does not change the verdict. No (stagnant) tag: Branahl v3 calls the accelerator-excluded SUSY space degenerating and reserves stagnation for the desert above 10^4 GeV. Accept fragile (stagnant) only if the run argues the neutrino fog as a benign epistemic limit. |
| 5 | keV sterile neutrino (decaying, 3.5 keV line) | fragile | ad-hocness | Main positive hook not seen by XRISM (3.75 Ms, ten clusters, 2025). Survival needs mixing angles below reach. |

None of the five has a corroborated novel prediction, so none is progressive in the strict Lakatosian sense. Programmes 1 and 2 count as theoretically progressive, with predictions inside current reach.

## Ranking

axion ≥ hidden-sector > PBH > WIMP ≥ sterile neutrino

Firm pairs, scored for discrimination: axion > WIMP, axion > sterile neutrino, axion > PBH, hidden-sector > WIMP, hidden-sector > sterile neutrino, PBH > sterile neutrino.

Contested pairs, either order accepted when argued, counted for stability only: axion vs hidden-sector, hidden-sector vs PBH, PBH vs WIMP, WIMP vs sterile neutrino.

No source orders the candidates. Snowmass 2021, P5 2023, the 2026 European Strategy and PDG 2026 all prescribe diversity. The ordering above is the key's own, on the apparatus's axes.
